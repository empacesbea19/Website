Favorite Place - Tokyo, Japan


Our favorite place is Tokyo, Japan. We were once an anime fan, and that is one of the reasons we became interested in Japan. It is a modern and exciting city known for its advanced technology, tall buildings, and rich culture. We like Tokyo because it has both old temples and modern buildings. The food looks delicious, and the streets are always lively. We also love the view of Mount Fuji and the cherry blossoms in spring because they make the city look so beautiful. Visiting Tokyo is one of our dreams because it seems both peaceful and full of life at the same time.
